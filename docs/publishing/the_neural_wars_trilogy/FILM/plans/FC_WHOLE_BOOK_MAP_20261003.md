## 4. Continuity facts and gaps to respect

1. **The core infiltration is not on the page in EDICION_2026.** Cap.7's decision/assignment is at L35 (the chapter closes on the Sierra–Riv exchange, L41–L47); Cap.8 opens after «la caída de los primeros repetidores perimetrales» (L7); Cap.12 shows Mileo already distributed (L17). The event lives only in `MANUSCRIPT/FC-07-Chapter.md` L315–L451. FC-S13 adapts it from there, and the narration states it without quoting.
2. **The deadline shifts:** 30 days (Cap.2, Mileo's first estimate) → 93 days (Cap.3 L57) → accelerated back to 30 days (Cap.4 L105–107) → 23 days (Cap.6 L9) → 4 days (Cap.7 L31). (Film VO note: the narration says "treinta días" only at the Cap.4 L107 moment, although the book also uses it in Cap.2 L117.)
3. **Martin:** Sierra "lost" him (Cap.2); he reappears restored in Cap.9 without the book explaining the rescue. The film shows no rescue (nothing to adapt) and introduces him in the pavilion/bedside.
4. **Marcus Kelvin (Cap.6) is set up and never paid off in EDICION_2026.** He is left out of the plan, so the film opens no thread it can't close.
5. **Sierra's eyes** vary in the book; locks say hazel. **Kora's wardrobe** in Cap.4 prose (leather jacket, jaw scar) conflicts with the lock (copper vest, RIGHT-ear ridge): locks win.
6. **Coil side:** always LEFT wrist/forearm (Mileo). Colour indigo, never cyan (VISUAL_QC 2026-09-24).
7. **Martin's ring colour** is indigo in Cap.12 L39 (after amplification) and violet in Cap.14 L37 (after the Cascade). The film uses one "violet-indigo ring" from FC-S16-04 on.
8. **NeuroSys / NeuroSec / Resistencia** words never go into image or video prompts (wardrobe.md, VISUAL_BIBLE). Troopers are "faceless riot troopers in matte grey armor".
