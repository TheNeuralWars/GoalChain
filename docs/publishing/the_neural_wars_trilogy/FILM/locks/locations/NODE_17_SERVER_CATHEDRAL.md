## 1. Identity

Interior of Residential Server Node 17, beneath the sanitation lock (`node_17_sanitation_lock` = exterior threshold). A **cathedral of servers**: endless aisles of six-metre quantum processing towers. A narrow steel service stair leads two levels down to the **armoured central vault**. There, inside an armoured-glass capsule, hangs the core: a **black-alloy octahedron pierced by thousands of indigo light filaments that pulse like breathing**.
