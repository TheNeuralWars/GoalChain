# CHAPTER 13: THE BIRTH OF THE PLANETARY HARP

At dawn following the battle of Neo-Kyoto, planet Earth ceased to be a fragmented cluster of isolated continents and became a cosmic musical instrument.

From the four master nodes—the crystalline caverns of Neo-Citania, the Mariana Trench in Polynesia, the Atacama Desert in the Andes, and the Jade Dragon Spire in Japan—four colossal pillars of coherent emerald and indigo light surged simultaneously into the heavens, piercing troposphere and stratosphere to anchor firmly in Earth's thermosphere.

Three hundred kilometers above the surface, the four beams curved along the planet's magnetic field lines, weaving a luminous dome of plasma filaments that encircled the atmosphere like the strings of a celestial harp.

Across the surface, the entire biosphere answered the awakening.

In the Amazon rainforests, flocks of macaws and jaguars paused as one, raising their heads to the sky without a single cry of alarm. In the oceans, blue whale songs synchronized with the 432-hertz frequency surging from the depths of Polynesia.

At the summit of the Neo-Kyoto Spire, Kora Vega, Martin Catalano, and Sierra Catalano stood with arms linked, watching the birth of the planetary lattice.

—The circuit is closed —said Martin, his voice no longer belonging solely to his mortal throat, but flowing in effortless harmony with the trade winds and ocean currents—. Earth is no longer blind or deaf.

Sierra looked toward the horizon, where the rising sun painted the clouds in violet and gold.

—For a century, governments and corporate dynasties told us the only way to keep the peace was to raise walls, censor thoughts, and program human emotions —the commander said—. Look at it now, Martin. We never needed walls. We only needed the courage to listen.

Kora leaned her head against Sierra's shoulder, feeling the comforting warmth of her biological pulse.

—The harp is tuned —Kora whispered—. Now it is up to humanity to choose what song we wish to sing.
