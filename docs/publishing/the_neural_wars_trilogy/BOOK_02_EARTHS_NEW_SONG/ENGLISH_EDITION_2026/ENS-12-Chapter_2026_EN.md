# CHAPTER 12: THE BATTLE OF NEO-KYOTO

The night sky over Neo-Kyoto burned in a hellish storm of plasma tracers, thermal countermeasure flares, and the white-hot arcs of heavy electromagnetic pulse bursts.

Rising three hundred meters above the fire-streaked waters of the bay stood the **Jade Dragon Spire**: the most formidable quantum transmission node in the Asian hemisphere, and the sole uplink capable of binding the frequencies of Atacama, Polynesia, and Neo-Citania into a unified ionospheric broadcast.

Twelve heavy gunships from the Asian Military Consortium circled the bay in attack formation, hammering the tower's thermal containment shields with relentless volleys of high-yield EMP warheads.

On the spire's open maintenance platform, amidst a howling squall of titanium shrapnel and acrid chemical smoke, Sierra Catalano and Riv fought to keep the primary array alive.

—The antenna shields are down to eighteen percent! —Riv roared, his hands blistered from overheating bypass relays as he welded an emergency shunting cable with a plasma torch—. If another heavy warhead hits the primary inductor coil, the spire collapses and the entire eastern hemisphere goes dark!

Sierra emptied the magazine of her assault rifle into a squad of combat androids attempting to breach the platform from a drop-shuttle. Three machines plummeted into the black water below in a shower of sparks.

—Hold the line for two more minutes, Riv! —Sierra ordered, reloading on the move as freezing sea rain lashed her smoke-streaked face—. Kora's airborne strike team is crossing the Tsushima Strait!

In that second, a corporate battlecruiser descended into low hover directly above the spire, opening the armored bay doors of its orbital railgun for the kill shot.

The air ionized with a lethal electrostatic crackle.

And then, sea and sky erupted in a blinding flash of indigo fire.

Surging through the storm clouds, the quantum submarine *Nautilus-7* broke the bay's surface, firing a colossal acoustic resonance pulse that scrambled the battlecruiser's repulsor drives. At the same instant, Kora Chen-Vega leapt from a low-flying assault skiff, executing a combat slide across the rain-slicked platform.

Dropping to her knees at the base of the master inductor, Kora drove the Atacama crystal artifact directly into the antenna's primary core.

A massive shockwave of pure Cascade consciousness detonated from the spire, sweeping across the bay in a widening halo of violet light.

The targeting suites on the enemy gunships died instantly. The assault androids froze in place like iron statues. And inside the helmets of the attacking pilots, there was no fatal electrical surge: there was an ocean of clear, radiant, heartbreaking music that drew silent tears from their eyes and stayed their trigger fingers.

The Jade Dragon Spire had held.

The network was now anchored across the four corners of the Earth. And humanity was ready to sing its final song.
