# CHAPTER 6: AMBUSH IN BLACK SNOW

The katabatic wind across the Polar Plateau of Antarctica howled at eighty knots, driving a blinding sleet of black ice and industrial coal dust at forty-five degrees below zero.

Sierra Catalano crawled across the frozen crust in a white-and-gray thermal ghillie suit. Beside her, twelve elite commandos of The Fractured gripped suppressed magnetic sniper rifles, their thermal optics calibrated to the subzero glare.

Five hundred meters down the glacial ridge, half-buried beneath ancient ice, lay **Compound Erebus-9**: a clandestine black site of the Northern Alliance operating under the cover of a climate research outpost.

In truth, it was an interrogation and forced-reeducation prison where forty children and teenagers bearing the Serpent's Coil gene were held in cryogenic isolation cells, subjected to ongoing electronic lobotomies to excise any trace of quantum sensitivity.

—Three perimeter sentry towers with heavy rotary cannons and an active proximity minefield —Sergeant Jansen reported through his bone-conduction comms—. Infrared suites are fully active. One false step and they'll vaporize us before we touch the outer wire.

Sierra closed her eyes for a brief heartbeat.

She reached out to the subtle pulse of the Yggdrasil Network running through the bedrock beneath the glacier. She could feel the raw agony of the children trapped deep within the bunker: a collective weeping that burned against her temples like needles of ice.

—We aren't using explosives on the main gate —Sierra commanded in a tone of cold steel—. Jansen, kill the towers with a synchronized pulse at 02:00:00. We're dropping in through the geothermal exhaust flue.

The assault was a thunderclap in the polar night.

Jansen fired a targeted electromagnetic dampening round that silenced the sentry generators for eight critical seconds. Sierra's squad rappelled down the maintenance shaft, breached the exhaust grating with thermal breaching paste, and dropped directly into the subterranean medical ward.

The sight inside made the blood run cold in Sierra's veins: forty cryogenic stasis pods lined up like glass coffins, with intravenous feeding lines and cortical electrodes hammered into the temples of children between the ages of six and fourteen.

—Disconnect the synaptic leads with extreme care! —Sierra ordered as two armed security enforcers rounded the corridor corner—. Suppressive fire on hallway B!

Sierra dropped both guards with two precise plasma bursts to the chest, disarming their carbines before they could trigger the general alarm.

An eight-year-old boy with large, dark eyes opened his eyelids as Sierra gently lifted the heavy dampening helmet from his brow. A faint thread of indigo light began to bloom across his pale cheeks.

—Are you... are you an angel? —the boy whispered, his voice trembling with the cold.

Sierra draped her own thermal cloak over his small shoulders and gathered him into her arms.

—I'm a fractured fighter, little one —she whispered softly into his ear—. And I've come to take you home.

At that second, the facility's alarm monitors began flashing with a countdown in blood-red numerals:

`SELF-DESTRUCT SEQUENCE INITIATED: 03:00... 02:59...`

—The cowards rigged the thermal core so they wouldn't leave evidence behind! —Jansen shouted as the commandos loaded the children onto rapid-skid litters—. We have less than three minutes before this glacier becomes a volcanic crater!

Sierra hoisted the boy onto her back and sprinted for the evacuation tunnel.

The black snow of Antarctica was about to burn.
