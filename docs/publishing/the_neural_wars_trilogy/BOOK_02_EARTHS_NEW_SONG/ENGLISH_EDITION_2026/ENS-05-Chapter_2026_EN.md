# CHAPTER 5: THE SUNKEN CITIES

The tactical submersible *Nautilus-7* glided six hundred meters deep through the Mariana Trench, in the dark abyss of the Pacific Ocean.

Inside the cockpit, the silence was almost absolute, broken only by the rhythmic hiss of the carbon scrubber and the low murmur of abyssal currents brushing against the titanium hull. Riv piloted with one hand on the analog helm and the other resting on his left thigh, where the jagged thermal scars still reminded him of his narrow escape in Sector 7.

Ahead of them, through the thick reinforced quartz viewports, the pitch-black ocean floor began to fracture with thousands of shimmering emerald and violet lights: the **Polynesian Abyssal Metropolis**.

It was neither an offshore military outpost nor a deep-sea drilling station: it was a sprawling cluster of hundreds of transparent biopolymer geodesic domes anchored to geothermal vents, linked by flexible glass transit tubes.

Within the domes, thousands of engineers, marine biologists, and fishermen who had fled corporate purges had developed a unique adaptation of the Serpent's Coil gene: their synaptic bioluminescence was not confined to their skin, but propagated through surrounding water molecules, allowing them to communicate in a silent language of light pulses and acoustic pressure waves.

Kora Chen-Vega gazed at the submerged city, her eyes wide with wonder.

—The Yggdrasil Network doesn't just travel through air or stone, Riv —Kora whispered, feeling the briny caress of thousands of interconnected minds drifting through the ocean currents—. Water is the ultimate quantum conductor.

The docking collar locked with a heavy metallic clank. The hatch hissed open, and a woman in her early fifties, her salt-weathered skin framing silver hair braided with glowing optical filaments, stepped forward to greet them.

—I am Captain Moana Silva —she introduced herself, opening both hands in the traditional greeting of the Ocean Clans—. We have been awaiting your arrival for three days. The 432-hertz signal has caused humpback whales and giant squid shoals to alter their migratory routes. The entire ocean is awake.

Kora opened the titanium briefcase that Dr. Naomi Lang had saved from the North Sea and retrieved a tray of fifty Serenity Filters.

—We need to couple these filters to the acoustic transducers on the thermal chimneys —Kora explained—. If we synchronize the Polynesian node with the crystalline caverns of Neo-Citania, we will forge a bio-aquatic antenna array capable of covering the entire Southern Hemisphere.

Moana took one of the crystal microchips, holding it up against the ambient light of bioluminescent jellyfish drifting outside the dome.

—The Asian Consortium has deployed unmanned surface destroyers above us —the captain warned gravely—. They have been dropping active sonar depth charges to shatter our domes and force a network blackout. If we power up the thermal transducers, their hydrophone arrays will pinpoint our position in less than ten minutes.

Riv stepped up to the navigational table, leaning heavily on his knuckles.

—Ten minutes underwater with my electromagnetic pulse charges is a lifetime, Captain. Give us access to the turbine room. If those warships want a sonar duel, we're about to teach them how the ocean floor sings.
