# CHAPTER 4: THE FLIGHT OF THE PALE FALCON

The North Sea roared under a violent gale of ten-meter swells and salt spray at three degrees below zero.

Forty miles off the Norwegian coastline, the deep-sea research rig *Njord-4* swayed atop its massive hydrodynamic pontoons. Once an offshore drilling installation, it had been repurposed by **Dr. Naomi Lang** into the most advanced scientific sanctuary of the free resistance.

Inside the main laboratory, the hum of three hundred quantum resonance processors battled against the thunder of the storm rattling the reinforced bay windows.

Naomi held a tiny micro-chip of crystallized silicon and organic polymer between her gloved fingers—no larger than an eyelash: the first working prototype of the **Serenity Filters**.

—If the harmonic calibration holds —Naomi explained to her team of six refugee scientists from the Alliance—, this device, placed behind the ear, acts as a synaptic refraction prism. It allows any human bearing the Serpent's Coil gene to receive the 432-hertz cosmic transmission without their dopaminergic pathways suffering catastrophic sensory overload. It translates the cosmic roar into an intelligible whisper.

A young engineer monitored the telemetry.

—Testing interface coupling on test subject twelve... Synaptic resonance optimal. Heart rate holding steady at seventy beats per minute. Zero nasal hemorrhaging, zero motor spasms. Doctor, it works! The filter stabilizes the incoming feed at ninety-nine percent!

A tired smile of triumph broke across Naomi's face. For the first time in months of exile and flight, they possessed a tool capable of shielding millions of innocent civilians from psychological dissolution.

In that instant, the laboratory's tactical consoles strobed in furious crimson.

`PROXIMITY WARNING: SIX UNIDENTIFIED AIRBORNE VECTORS`  
`VELOCITY: MACH 4.2 // TRAJECTORY: DIRECT DIVE ON NJORD-4`

—Heavy assault strike craft! —the radar operator shouted—. No national insignia, no civil transponders! They're unmanned *Pale Falcon* stealth interceptors belonging to the Euro-Siberian Consortium!

The rig's sirens wailed into the stormy night.

Through the sheeting rain, six razor-sharp, triangular craft shattered the sound barrier over the rig, unleashing a salvo of guided thermobaric missiles straight into the stabilization pontoons.

The explosions tore through the port hull. The entire rig groaned and tilted violently as freezing black seawater surged into the lower cargo decks.

—They're trying to destroy the servers and seize the prototypes! —Naomi shouted, snapping the three trays of Serenity Filters into a sealed titanium briefcase—. Initiate the automated zero-fill purge on the mainframes! Leave not a single byte of the formula for the enemy!

The reinforced laboratory doors blew inward in a thunderous breach of plastic explosives.

Three mercenaries in pressurized combat armor and black visors stormed the smoking chamber, suppressed assault rifles raised.

—Dr. Naomi Lang —the squad commander growled through a vocal filter—. Hand over the case and step forward. Your research is now the property of Unified Corporate Security.

Naomi looked at the briefcase, then at her young assistants shielding their heads on the floor, and finally at the shattered window looking out over the churning black abyss of the sea.

—My research belongs to the sovereign freedom of the human mind —Naomi replied with icy calm.

In a blinding flash of movement, she hurled a white-phosphorus grenade straight into the primary electrical panels.

The explosion erupted in an inferno of white fire, instantly vaporizing the power grid and engulfing the mercenaries in a blinding vortex of smoke and sparks. Naomi leapt through the shattered bay window, diving headfirst into the freezing surge of the North Sea, clutching the briefcase that held the salvation of humanity against her chest.

And in the pitch-black depths below, the bioluminescent running lights of a stealth submersible piloted by Kora and Riv rose through the dark water to catch her.
