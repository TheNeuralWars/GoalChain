PROLOGUE

The rain falls in sevens.

Mileo Chen has counted it for six years — the pattern the meteorological grid uses when it wants to look natural — and tonight the sevens are off by a tenth of a second. He stands at the window of unit 4407 with his palm flat on cold glass and does not move.

Behind him the Link hums its evening compliance: pulse 58, hydration 91%, mood green.

He is a Level 7 neural compliance specialist. He has signed four hundred and eleven clean bills of mental health for people he has never met, and he has never once been wrong on paper.

Under his hand, the condensation is thinning in the shape of a door.

His pulse stays at 58. That is the thing about the Link: it will not let you be afraid. It has to ask first.

At 03:14 the grid pauses. Twelve thousand windows go dark in a single breath, and in the silence Mileo Chen hears, for the first time in his life, eight million minds being counted.
