Privacy.

Plain version: this website collects no personal data unless you choose to join the waitlist. Here are the details.

The website (goalworld.fun)

  • It is a static site with no accounts. The only form is the optional waitlist.
  • Waitlist: if you join, we store your email address, the signup time and an approximate country (from Cloudflare). We keep these in Cloudflare Workers KV and send a confirmation email through Resend. We use your email only to send GoalWorld updates. We never sell or share it. To be removed, email nico@goalworld.fun.
  • It sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Fonts and images are served from goalworld.fun itself, not from third-party CDNs.
  • Like any web server, the hosting infrastructure may keep standard technical logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) for security and reliability. These are used for operations only.

The game (play.goalworld.fun)

  • GoalChain Play is a demo that runs in your browser. Game state — profile, balance, predictions, inventory — is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • You may connect a Phantom wallet on Solana Devnet. The demo does not execute real on-chain transactions and does not request transfers of real funds.
  • We do not ask for, and you should never send, private keys, seed phrases or passwords to anyone.

Third-party links

This site links to X, GitHub and other external services. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave goalworld.fun.

Children

GoalWorld is a sci-fi saga and a football game demo with no age-gated content or purchases. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.
