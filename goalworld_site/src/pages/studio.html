Case study #1

Fractured Code: draft → book.

Phase 1

Diagnose & redesign

A full structural read, then a story memo the author approves before a single line is rewritten.

Phase 2

Rewrite & line edit

Chapter by chapter, against the series bible. The draft grew from about 17k to about 74k words.

Phase 3

Canon & final read

Dates, ages, names and physics checked across the whole book. Open questions go back to the author, not into the text.

Every gate is signed by the author. The voice stays theirs.

Living Canon

Dynamic cuts. One strict canon.

A director model writes a scene for a specific audience — a teen gamer in Italian, a film buff in English, a plain-language viewer. CanonGuard checks it against the world's rules and repairs anything that breaks.

Wrong wrist, wrong scar, a logo sneaking into a shot: caught and fixed before it ships. Built on open models.
