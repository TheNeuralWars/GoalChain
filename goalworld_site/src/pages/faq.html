FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers about the books, the game, the demo, and the people building it.

What is GoalWorld?

GoalWorld — World of Achievements — is the world layer of a single project: the books and film of The Neural Wars, the football game GoalChain Play, and the studio that produces both. One brand, one world, built in the open.

What is The Neural Wars?

A hard sci-fi / cyberpunk saga. Book 1, Fractured Code, is set in Neo-Citania, a city where every mind is connected to the Link — until a compliance specialist named Mileo Chen hears the truth at 03:14. It is written, it is real, and you can read it free in the GoalWorld reader.

Where can I read Fractured Code?

Right here: goalworld.fun/go/reader. No wallet, no signup, no email. The 2026 definitive English edition is complete, and a Spanish edition is in the works.

What is GoalChain Play?

GoalChain is the matchday engine: a football game you own, built around World Cup 2026. You create a manager, build a squad from the Genesis collection, play matches in the Arena with real tactics and substitutions, and call the tournament with the Predictor. It lives at play.goalworld.fun.

Do I need crypto or real money to play?

No. GoalChain Play is a playable demo running on Solana Devnet. It does not execute real on-chain transactions, and it never requires real capital — the app is clearly marked wherever a section is simulation only.

What is the $GCH token I see in the app?

It is the in-app demo token of the GoalChain simulation — used for match entry, boosts and rewards inside the game. It has no market value, no sale, and no real-money flow. Anything token-related that ships for real will be announced from the official channels, never sprung inside the demo.

What wallet do I need?

The demo connects with Phantom on Solana Devnet. You can also browse the world, the books and the cinema without any wallet at all.

Is the film out?

The official cinematic trailer and film-draft clips are published here and in the cinema section. The full Fractured Code film is still in production — new chapters are cut and posted as the pipeline produces them.

Who is building this?

Nico Pez (@nicopez) with a swarm of AI agents, working in the open. The code, the roadmaps and the production notes live on GitHub.

How do I get in touch?

The fastest way is a DM on X to @nicopez. For anything code or product related, GitHub is always open.
