AI cinema

The book, on screen.

Every shot starts as a line in the novel. The faces, places and colours are locked before a single frame is generated. These are drafts — made with AI, directed by a human, labelled every time.

Full draft conform (~6.8 min) and new scenes post here as they clear review.

How we make it

Canon in. Cinema out.

Lock the canon

Character lock sheets, a colour bible and a list of every place. Scars, wrists and eyes don't move.

Book to shots

Each scene is broken into shots traced back to a line of the novel. One camera, one action per shot.

Generate

An approved still first, then image-to-video. Agents on our server run the queue.

Check

A continuity guard compares faces to the locks and flags drift. Failing shots get regenerated.

Cut & score

A human edit, AI voices and music, subtitles. Every tool disclosed.

Gate

Nothing goes public until the director says so. AI is the crew, not the author.
