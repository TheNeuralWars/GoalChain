## 2. THE BOKEH & ANTI-DISTRACTION PROTOCOLS (PREVENTING CLUTTER)
To prevent the background from clashing with the player card assets, you **MUST** strictly adhere to the following composition rules:

1. **HEAVY DEPTH OF FIELD (MANDATORY)**:
   - Always describe the background as having `"extremely heavy depth of field, with the entire background wall strongly out of focus, creating a soft, buttery, professional bokeh blur"`.
   - Use photographic anchors like `"shot with a premium 50mm lens at a wide-open aperture of f/1.2 to dissolve background details into a soft texture"`.
2. **SOBER CONTRAST & MUTED VALUES**:
   - The overall background value must be dark (pitch charcoal, matte black, obsidian). 
   - Never use pure bright white or highly reflective materials. 
   - Reflected light must be described as `"subtle, matte, diffused, and low-contrast, casting a soft, gentle gradient on the floor with zero glare"`.
3. **SPARSE GRAPHIC ELEMENTS (ANTI-NOISE)**:
   - Keep details sparse. Avoid complex grids, thick glowing lines, or neon explosions. 
   - Use terms like `"abstract, extremely sparse, ultra-thin, low-intensity recessed LED lines"` or `"soft, dim, barely-visible volumetric atmospheric haze"`.
