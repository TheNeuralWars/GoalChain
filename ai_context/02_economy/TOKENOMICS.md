## 2. The Infinity Engine (Vault Mechanics)
GoalChain operates on a **Zero Value Loss** philosophy. Capital is never liquidated; it is put to work.

### A. The Vault Injection
- **100% of Primary Sales** (Minting, Mystery Packs) is deposited directly into **Solana Liquid Staking** (JitoSOL, mSOL).
- **50% of Marketplace Fees** is added to the Vault.

### B. Real Yield Buy-back & Burn
The Vault generates **7% - 10% APY** in SOL. This yield is used exclusively to:
1. **Buy-back $GCH** from Decentralized Exchanges (DEX).
2. **Burn** a portion of the bought $GCH to create deflationary pressure.
3. **Distribute** the remaining as "Salaries" to NFT holders.

## 3. Revenue Distribution (5-2-3 Split)
Every economic activity (Staking Yield, Market Fees, Betting House Edge) is split as follows:
- **50% - Community Jackpot:** Incentives for the Penalty Shootout and Betting Pools.
- **20% - Player Salaries:** Distributed to NFT holders based on their Squad performance.
- **30% - Treasury & Development:** Fueling the "Vision 2027" roadmap and DAO operations.

## 4. Manager Career & LP Staking
Managers can boost their earnings by participating in the liquidity of the ecosystem.
- **Salary Boost:** Providing $GCH/SOL liquidity (LP) grants a stacking multiplier to player salaries.
- **Manager Level:** Experience points (XP) earned from gameplay unlock higher roster limits and exclusive "Manager Licenses".

## 5. Deflationary Mechanisms
- **The Burn Tracker:** Real-time visibility of $GCH destruction funded by the Vault.
- **Card Fusion:** Burning common prospects to upgrade NFT attributes or rarity.
- **Entry Fees:** A portion of mini-game entry fees is permanently removed from circulation.
