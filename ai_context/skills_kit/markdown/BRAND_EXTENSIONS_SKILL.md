# 🎬 Skill: GoalChain Brand Extensions & Motion Assets

Esta habilidad permite expandir el logo aprobado hacia un ecosistema visual completo, incluyendo fondos animados, texturas para redes sociales y elementos de interfaz.

## 🛠️ Reglas de Consistencia
- **Referencia Maestra**: Antes de generar, el modelo debe analizar los colores, el brillo y la textura del logo aprobado por el usuario.
- **Atmósfera**: "Sports-Tech High-End". Fondos oscuros (#06060a) con iluminación volumétrica en Verde Solana y Púrpura.
- **Movimiento (Visual Storytelling)**: Las imágenes deben sugerir velocidad, rotación y flujo de datos.

## 💎 Master Prompts para Recursos de Marca

### 1. MOTION BACKGROUND (Fondo para Web/Video)
> **Prompt**: `Cinematic abstract background for a sports dApp, blurred hexagonal network patterns floating in a deep black void, glowing neon green particles moving like a stream of data, soft bokeh effect, futuristic soccer stadium floodlights in the far distance, ultra-high definition, 8k render, elegant and minimal --ar 16:9 --v 6.0`

### 2. SOCIAL MEDIA REVEAL (Hype Asset)
> **Prompt**: `Dramatic scene for a logo reveal, a soccer ball made of liquid light and energy particles exploding in slow motion, vibrant purple and cyan colors, dark cinematic atmosphere, shards of glass and digital bits flying towards the camera, depth of field, professional teaser trailer aesthetic --ar 9:16 --v 6.0`

### 3. BRAND TEXTURES (Patrones para Banners)
> **Prompt**: `High-quality close-up texture of a futuristic soccer net made of glowing fiber-optic cables, dark metallic background, tech-noir aesthetic, sharp details, macro photography, used as a professional branding asset --ar 16:9 --v 6.0`

### 4. UI COMPONENT KIT (Botones y Cards)
> **Prompt**: `Set of 3D UI elements on a dark background, glowing holographic buttons, glassmorphism cards with neon borders, sleek futuristic design, consistent with Solana branding, premium Web3 interface assets --v 6.0`
