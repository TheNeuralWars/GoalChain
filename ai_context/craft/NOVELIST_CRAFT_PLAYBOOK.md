## 🔍 The 7-Point Editorial Quality Gate (The Pre-Flight Check)

Before any chapter or scene is finalized, run it through this checklist:

1. [ ] **Acoustic Sentence Variance**: Are there staccato strikes (1–5 words) interspersed with expansive sensory sweeps? Is the rhythm musical?
2. [ ] **Terminal Stress**: Do sentences finish on heavy, concrete nouns or punchy verbs rather than weak prepositions or em-dash tails?
3. [ ] **Sensory Triangulation**: Does the scene engage at least one somatic, one chemical/olfactory, and one tactile texture?
4. [ ] **Zero Filter Words**: Are `felt`, `saw`, `heard`, `noticed`, and `wondered` completely purged?
5. [ ] **Zero Mechanical Tics**: Are clichéd somatic reactions (`swallowed hard`, `clenched jaw`, `breath he didn't know he was holding`) eradicated?
6. [ ] **Armed Dialogue**: Does every line of dialogue pursue an objective, withhold a secret, or contest territory? Is blocking dynamic?
7. [ ] **Causal Momentum**: Are beats linked by *therefore* and *but*, completely free of episodic *and then* slack?
