# 🖋️ Editorial & Literary Suite: Bestseller & Cinematic Masterclass

The `editorial-suite` skill empowers AI agents to write, rewrite, edit, analyze, and adapt narrative prose and worldbuilding lore at the caliber of award-winning fiction authors and Hollywood showrunners.

---

## 🏛️ I. Core Philosophy: The Living Prose Manifesto

Great fiction is not an exchange of informational facts; it is an **orchestrated visceral hallucination** in the reader's nervous system. Every line of prose must earn its place on the page through rhythm, emotional stakes, and sensory resonance.

### 1. The Anti-AI-Slop Blacklist
Never use the following cliché, formulaic, or hollow phrases that instantly identify generic AI writing:
* ❌ *"A testament to..."* / *"A symphony of..."* / *"A tapestry of..."*
* ❌ *"Palpable tension"* / *"Thick enough to cut with a knife"*
* ❌ *"A shiver ran down her spine"* / *"Sent chills down his spine"*
* ❌ *"Unbeknownst to them"* / *"Little did they know"*
* ❌ *"The sheer magnitude/audacity of..."*
* ❌ *"Labyrinthine corridors"* / *"Echoed hollowly"*
* ❌ *"Delve into"* / *"Beacon of hope"* / *"Fractured reality"* (unless used as a strict in-world proper noun)

### 2. Sentence Rhythm: The 5-Voice Cadence
Avoid monotonous sentence lengths (12–18 words repeating endlessly). Alternate rhythm intentionally:
* **The Staccato Strike (1–5 words)**: For kinetic shocks, revelations, and physical violence. (*"The cable snapped. Cold silence."*)
* **The Narrative Drive (8–15 words)**: For forward action, tactical movement, and direct interaction.
* **The Expansive Flow (25–40+ words)**: For atmospheric immersion, philosophical shifts, and sensory sweeps, using balanced hypotaxis (dependent clauses) and sharp, concrete nouns.

### 3. Elimination of Filter Words
Remove the narrator’s middleman. Drop the character's observational filters:
* ❌ *Weak (Filtered)*: "She felt the cold wind biting her cheeks. She heard the distant siren wailing."
* ✅ *Visceral (Direct)*: "Freezing wind bit her cheekbones. Three blocks east, an assault siren wailed."
* *Filters to purge*: `felt`, `saw`, `heard`, `noticed`, `wondered`, `seemed`, `appeared`, `watched`.

### 4. 5-Sense Grounding Matrix
Every major scene must engage at least **3 sensory channels**:
1. **Tactile/Somatic**: Temperature, pressure, texture, muscle strain, adrenaline tingling in teeth/fingers.
2. **Olfactory/Gustatory**: Ozone, copper/blood on the tongue, scorched plastic, damp stone, rain on hot asphalt.
3. **Auditory**: Sub-bass vibrations, rhythmic machine hums, wet footsteps, breath catching in throats.
4. **Visual Contrast**: Light temperature (amber vs violet), specular reflections, shadows, biological eye dilation.

## 🎬 II. Narrative Architecture: The Cinematic 4-Act Engine

Every novel and screenplay adaptation follows the **4-Act / 8-Sequence** narrative curve:

```
[ ACT I: THE WORLD AS IS ] (25%)
  ├─ Seq 1: Status Quo & The Flaw
  └─ Seq 2: Inciting Incident & Crossing the Threshold (Plot Point 1)

[ ACT II-A: THE EXPLORATION OF THE NEW REALITY ] (25%)
  ├─ Seq 3: The Promise of the Premise & Allies
  └─ Seq 4: Midpoint Reversal (False Victory / Crushing Defeat & High Stakes)

[ ACT II-B: THE CONVERGENCE OF FORCES ] (25%)
  ├─ Seq 5: The Walls Close In & Antagonist Counter-Offensive
  └─ Seq 6: The Dark Night of the Soul / All Hope Lost (Plot Point 2)

[ ACT III: THE CLIMACTIC CONVERGENCE ] (25%)
  ├─ Seq 7: The Breakthrough, Final Battle & Transformation
  └─ Seq 8: Resolution, Epilogue & The Cosmic New Normal
```

### Scene-and-Sequel Mechanics
Ensure structural pacing never drags:
1. **Scene (Active Goal)**:
   * **Goal**: Character enters with a specific, immediate objective.
   * **Conflict**: Obstacles, opposing willpower, environmental resistance.
   * **Disaster (The Hook)**: The attempt fails, succeeds at a terrible cost, or reveals a worse danger.
2. **Sequel (Reactive Processing)**:
   * **Reaction**: Visceral somatic and emotional response.
   * **Dilemma**: No easy options; choosing between two painful paths.
   * **Decision**: Formulating the new proactive goal for the next scene.

## 🎭 III. Dialogue Subtext & Voice Distinctness

1. **Dialogue is Action**: Characters do not speak to relay exposition to the reader. They speak to threaten, seduce, deflect, extract information, conceal weakness, or assert dominance.
2. **The 3 Layers of Dialogue**:
   * *Text*: What is literally said.
   * *Subtext*: What is actually meant or feared.
   * *Blocking*: What the character's body is physically doing while speaking (drumming fingers, avoiding eye contact, checking sightlines).
3. **Voice Distinctness Matrix**:
   * *Mileo Chen*: Analytical, precise, surgical vocabulary, suppressed emotion breaking through in sudden poetic flashes.
   * *Kora Chen-Vega*: Street-smart, kinetic, sensory metaphors, biting humor, visceral defiance.
   * *Sierra Catalano*: Military brevity, tactical framing, emotional iron hiding profound grief.

## 🔍 IV. Editorial Audit Protocol

When reviewing or rewriting any chapter or scene, run the **4-Tier Audit**:

1. **Tier 1: Line Audit (Prose)**:
   - Check sentence length variance.
   - Strip adverbs modifying dialogue tags (*"he said angrily"* $\rightarrow$ *"he slammed his mug onto the table"*).
   - Purge AI clichés and filter words.
2. **Tier 2: Scene Dynamics (Stakes)**:
   - What did the protagonist want at the start? Did the scene change their status by the end?
   - Is there authentic conflict, or is the conversation polite and compliant?
3. **Tier 3: Canon & Worldbuilding Consistency**:
   - Are tech rules strictly adhered to (quantum decay, Link dampeners, 432 Hz frequencies)?
   - Are names, character histories, scars, and timeline milestones 100% aligned with the Series Bible?
4. **Tier 4: Bilingual Parity**:
   - Ensure the English and Spanish editions mirror each other paragraph by paragraph, nuance by nuance, without loss of literary richness.

## 📽️ V. Screenplay & Transmedia Adaptation Tools

For cinematic treatments, pitch decks, and showrunner bibles:
* **Logline Formula**: `When [INCITING INCIDENT OCCURS], a [FLAWED PROTAGONIST] must [TAKE PROACTIVE ACTION] against [FORMIDABLE ANTAGONIST] before [CATASTROPHIC STAKES].`
* **Scene Beats (Fountain Format)**: Break chapters into standard screenplay scenes with Sluglines (`INT. RESIDENTIAL TOWER - DAY`), Action lines (present tense, active verbs), and Dialogue.
* **Visual Asset Generation Prompts**: Cinematic lighting descriptors (anamorphic lens flare, chiaroscuro, volumetric smog, neon-indigo bioluminescence) for Midjourney/Imagen prompts.
