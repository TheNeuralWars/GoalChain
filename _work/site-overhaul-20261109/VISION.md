## 1. The Single Story: One Universe, Three Mediums

**GoalWorld** is an independent creative studio founded by writer and director **Nico Pez** (`@nicopez`), built in the open with an autonomous AI crew (**Hermes**, **CanonGuard**, **Antigravity**). Every project inside GoalWorld follows one strict creative order:

1. **CANON (Written First — *The Neural Wars*):** Everything begins as literature. Our flagship seven-book hard sci-fi saga, ***The Neural Wars***, opens in **2148 Neo-Citania** with **Book 1: *Fractured Code*** (~74,000 words, 16 chapters + epilogue, G2 editorial sign-off complete). When Neural Compliance Specialist **Mileo Chen** shatters his cranial **Link**, he joins unlinked operative **Kora Vega** and resistance commander **Sierra Catalano** in the Under-Grid to awaken a 300,000-year-old dormant human gene (**Serpent's Coil**, **528 Hz**) and stop **The Architect**’s **twenty-day** countdown to *Project Renaissance* across the 4.5-billion-year-old planetary **Yggdrasil Network**. **Book 2 (*Earth's New Song*)** is in active production; **Book 3 (*Evolution Matrix*)** is outlined.
2. **CINEMA (Directed by a Human, Rendered with AI):** Every shot on screen starts as a signed line in the novel. Using our 6-stage pipeline (*Lock the Canon → Book to Shots → Generate → CanonGuard Check → Cut & Score → Gate*), we produce concept trailers, keyframe studies, and narrative shorts. Every frame carries a visible **`AI-generated · Film draft`** disclosure.
3. **CODE (Living Canon & Matchday Lab):** We build the software that keeps worlds coherent and playable:
   - **Living Canon (`CanonGuard`):** Editorial and continuity verification tooling (`G0–G3` gates, automated continuity audit & repair) built for *Fractured Code* and opening later to a curated cohort of independent authors.
   - **Matchday Lab (`play.goalworld.fun`):** Our tactical football simulation sandbox running on **Solana Devnet** (bridging into the studio's ***AeroOlympus*** athletic-arena slate). Players draft squads, test match-engine telemetry, and experiment with verifiable digital provenance using **free Devnet test tokens (`$GCH`) with zero monetary value, zero financial risk, and nothing for sale**.

## 4. Voice, Tone & Localization Rules (`EN` / `ES` / `IT`)

- **Editorial Restraint (A24 / Annapurna Ethos):** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Zero hype adjectives, zero crypto-bro jargon, zero startup buzzwords. Let the prose, keyframes, and working software speak.
- **Radical Honesty:**
  - Explicitly distinguish **Live today**, **In production**, and **Provisional / Concept**.
  - **Hard Banned Claims (Zero Tolerance):** Never use `pre-order` / `prerreserva`, `available now`, `published on KDP`, `USDC royalties`, `yield`, `staking`, `Infinity Burn`, `Zero Value Loss`, `NFT market` (as real-money trading), `audited facial likeness`, `C2PA-verified` (until live), `$1M`, `Neo-Veridia`, `Kora Chen`, `seven days`, or `432 Hz` (for Serpent's Coil/wake resonance; canonical wake resonance is **528 Hz**, while **432 Hz** is only The Architect's sedative carrier when explicitly contrasted, and avoided in public marketing copy per `copy_deck.md`).
  - **Mandatory Disclosures:** Every AI video/still carries `AI-generated · Directed by Nico Pez`. Every `play.goalworld.fun` view carries a clear `Solana Devnet Sandbox · Free to play · No real money` indicator.
- **Trilingual Parity (`EN` primary, `ES` and `IT` first-class editions):**
  - Natural, literary English, Spanish, and Italian — never stiff machine translation.
  - **Canon Proper Nouns Stay Untranslated Across All Languages:** `GoalWorld`, `The Neural Wars`, `Fractured Code`, `Earth's New Song`, `Evolution Matrix`, `Neo-Citania`, `The Link`, `The Architect`, `Project Renaissance`, `Under-Grid`, `Serpent's Coil`, `Yggdrasil Network`, `Mileo Chen`, `Kora Vega`, `Sierra Catalano`, `Dr. Elena Vásquez`, `Matchday Lab`, `AeroOlympus`, `Aethelgard`, `CanonGuard`.
